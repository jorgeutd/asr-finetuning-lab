# Demo audio attribution

Source: [Google FLEURS](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs).

Authors: Alexis Conneau et al. (2022), *FLEURS: Few-shot Learning Evaluation of Universal Representations of Speech*.

License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Each language directory records the exact source revision, selected clip IDs, and dataset fingerprint in `provenance.json`. Audio is re-encoded as lossless FLAC. Transcripts use the source's raw transcription. Original split assignments are retained.

These small subsets demonstrate execution. They do not establish model quality or domain coverage.
